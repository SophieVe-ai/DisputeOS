ADVERTISING CONTRACT 2011
Contract reference: 2011 Advertising Contract
Contract date: 2010-12-24
Supplier: International Media Advertising Ltd
Customer: Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the Republic of Turkey
Parties: Consulate General of the Republic of Turkey, London Turkish Culture and Tourism Office, representing the Ministry of Culture and Tourism of the Republic of Turkey ("the Client"); dDf International Limited ("the Agency"); International Media Advertising ("Contracted Media Planning and Buying Partner")
Effective: 2011-01-01 to 2011-12-31 at the latest
Budget: USD 4.5 million for the 2011 Campaign (UK and Republic of Ireland)
Payment terms: The Client shall pay after the service has been received and all the necessary documents have been completely submitted. In case any of the above-mentioned documents are not submitted, no payment will be made to the Agency. In case payment is delayed for any reason, no interest shall be charged.
Pricing:
- Media buying, paid "over the net time/space cost (excluding commission and VAT)" per the approved media plan | qty 1
- Agency commission to DDF: 5 (five) % per country on the net media cost | qty 1

Article 2 (definition): The net media cost is the one submitted to the Client in the invoice approved by the medium in which advertisement is published.
Article 9 B): The Client is not responsible for making any advance payment, warranty or any kind of fee not written in this contract or not approved by the Client to the Agency within the framework of the approved media plan.
Article 9 D): The Contracted Media Planning and Buying Partner of the Agency shall confirm that all invoices submitted by third parties are correct and proper and, shall perform the necessary effort to ensure that the Client benefits from all types of discount. The Contracted Media Planning and Buying Partner of the Agency shall reflect all discounts exactly as given by the media buyers or the media companies, and will be responsible to provide the Client with all related documents and information.
Article 9 D) 1: The Agency or the Buying Partner shall send the detailed invoices (approved net media invoices issued by the media organization running the advertisement together with the invoices of the Buying Partner) which are addressed to the Client and conducted upon the net time/space cost of the advertisement, to the Client's Representative after the advertising has run. The invoice shall also include attachments of documents confirming the advertising has run.
Article 9 D) 2: The Agency must not request any additional expense other than the ones written in this contract.
Article 9 D) 3: The Agency should attach the Agency's Buying Partner's invoices together with certified copies of the net media invoices (received from the media organizations that run the ads).
Signed: not stated
