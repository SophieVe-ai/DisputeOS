# Addax Energy SA v Petro Trade Inc — [2023] EWHC 1609 (Comm)
- **Court / venue:** High Court, Business and Property Courts of England and Wales, London Circuit Commercial Court, Rolls Building, Fetter Lane, London EC4A 1NL (Claim No LM-2022-000140)
- **Judge:** David Elvin KC (sitting as a Deputy High Court Judge)
- **Hearing / hand-down:** Hearing 28 March 2023; handed down remotely 10 am, 4 July 2023
- **Source:** https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewhc/comm/2023/1609
- **Tier:** core: seller's claim on four unpaid invoices for fuel delivered into storage and released to the buyer; buyer (absent at trial) pleads no incorporated terms, theft of stock, risk not passed and non-acceptance; contractual interest awarded on a reduced basis the seller volunteered.

## Inputs
### Parties
- **Creditor / claimant:** Addax Energy SA, Swiss oil products trader, seller [para 2].
- **Debtor / defendant:** Petro Trade Inc ("PT"), Liberian importer and gas-station operator, buyer. PT challenged jurisdiction (dismissed by Cockerill J, [2022] EWHC 237 (Comm)), then de-instructed its solicitors and did not take part at trial [paras 2 to 7].
- **Non-party:** ACE Global Depository DMCC, collateral manager under the tripartite Secured Distribution Agreement; LPRC, state-owned operator of the storage tanks [paras 32, 55].

### Amounts
- **Invoiced total claimed:** US$2,761,408.78 (US$532,506.86 + US$2,228,901.92) [para 1].
- **Paid:** US$460,000 on account of the China Union gasoil [paras 59, 61].
- **Balance after correction:** US$2,744,174.93 (Gasoil Contract reduced to US$515,273.01 by the lower premium) [para 136].
- **Counterclaim / set-off:** set-off pleaded for ACE's alleged negligence; no counterclaim for return of the US$460,000 [paras 64(5), 65].
- **Interest claimed:** contractual under cl 10 (cost of funding plus 8%), but limited by Addax to a flat 8% p.a. and later start dates (Gasoil from 4 March 2021; Term Agreement from 4 December 2020) [paras 63, 131(5), 137].

### Debtor's defences and complaints
Defence served 31 March 2022; PT then took no further part [paras 3, 7].
1. **Spot contract terms not incorporated** (Gasoil Contract concluded orally; cl 10 interest not agreed) [para 64(1)].
2. **Stock appropriated / stolen:** Gasoil removed by Srimex between November 2016 and February 2017; Term Agreement balances removed in January or February 2020 [paras 64(5), (6), 124(7)].
3. **Risk and title remained with Addax; goods perished (s 7 Sale of Goods Act 1979) or contract frustrated; Addax liable as pledgee for ACE's negligence** [para 64(5), (7)].
4. **No acceptance, so only damages under s 50 SGA, not the price** [paras 64(8), 124(8)].
5. **Waiver / estoppel by convention** that PT would pay only as and when it took lots [para 64(9)].
6. **No binding Term Agreement:** 20 November 2018 document was only a draft; no agreed premium (agreement to agree); inconsistent prior terms; deliveries not per cl 7; cl 13 passed only quality risk [paras 124, 125].
7. **US$460,000 payments not on account of the Gasoil Contract** (no positive case) [para 64(4)].

## Outcome
### Decision on each defence
1. **Rejected:** consistent course of dealing over some five years incorporated the spot contract terms, including cl 10 and cl 13 [paras 88 to 90].
2. **Rejected:** no evidence from PT; authenticated stock reports show the product remained; PT's own letter of 3 February 2020 accepted responsibility after the alleged theft dates; Gray Page report was untested desk opinion [paras 101 to 106, 114, 115, 132].
3. **Rejected:** risk passed on DAP delivery at the flange (September 2016 and 2019); ACE was independent, so any claim lies against ACE; s 7 and frustration inapplicable [para 91].
4. **Rejected:** liability for the price turns on cl 10 (release), not acceptance; s 50 inapplicable [paras 90(4), 124(8)].
5. **Rejected:** no evidence of the common assumption or reliance; cl 10(II) clear [para 116].
6. **Rejected:** Term Agreement concluded orally on 3 January 2018 and recorded on 20 November 2018; PT never queried it; recaps ceased; premium mechanism reflected settled practice and is not fatal uncertainty; cl 13 passed risk generally [paras 129 to 131].
7. **Rejected:** payments made on account of unreleased gasoil, which shows PT accepted risk and liability [paras 93, 94].

### Money result
- PT liable for **US$2,744,174.93**: US$515,273.01 (Gasoil Contract) and US$2,228,901.92 (Term Agreement), plus interest until payment and costs [para 136].
- Interest to judgment at 8% p.a. [para 138]:
  - Gasoil: US$112.93 per day, 5 March 2021 to 13 April 2021 (US$4,157.20) plus 14 April 2021 to 4 July 2023 (US$91,699.16) = **US$95,856.36**.
  - Term Agreement: US$488.52 per day, 5 December 2020 to 13 April 2021 (US$63,507.60) plus 812 days (US$396,678.24) = **US$460,185.84**.
- Costs awarded to Addax; amount not stated.

## Commentary
- *Commentary:* The decisive evidence was the debtor's own conduct: US$460,000 paid on account for stock it later said had been stolen years earlier, and a signed letter of 3 February 2020 accepting responsibility for all stocks. Independent stock reports from the collateral manager then defeated the theft story.
- *Commentary:* Collections lesson: where goods sit in third-party storage and are released against payment, keep the stock reports, release orders and any written acknowledgement of responsibility; they prove both delivery and the passing of risk.
- *Commentary:* Challenge should reject "goods lost after delivery" defences once DAP delivery and a contractual risk-passing clause are shown, and redirect any loss claim to the storage operator. It should test incorporation of standard terms by counting how consistently recaps and spot contracts were sent and accepted.
- *Commentary:* Reconciliation should net on-account payments against the specific delivery, apply the corrected premium (US$43, not US$53 per tonne) and compute 8% simple interest per invoice from the conceded start dates. Possible error in the judgment: the Term Agreement second period is printed as "14/04/2021 to 28/03/2023" but uses 812 days, the same count as the Gasoil period ending 4 July 2023.
- *Commentary:* Resolution: the claimant limiting its interest to 8% and later start dates (rather than cost of funding plus 8% from earlier dates) helped present a fair case against an absent debtor; DisputeOS should model both strict contractual and conceded interest figures in the court-claim pack.
