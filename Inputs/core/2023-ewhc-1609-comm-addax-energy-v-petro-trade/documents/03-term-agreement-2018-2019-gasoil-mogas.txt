TERM AGREEMENT 2018 TO 2019 GASOIL MOGAS
Contract reference: Petro Trade - Term Contract 2018 to 2019 Gasoil Mogas
Contract date: 2018-01-03
Written terms sent: 2018-11-20
Supplier: Addax Energy SA
Customer: Petro Trade Inc
Currency: USD
Payment terms: ninety [90] calendar days from each release of product (products are discharged basis DAP Monrovia at the name of buyer, stored in Monrovia under a SMA supervised by ACE, and then released by lots of about 1000 MT to buyer) (cl. 10)
Risk and title: pass from seller to buyer upon delivery DAP Monrovia (cl. 13)
Pricing:

- Mogas prem unl 10ppm | qty not stated | Platt's FOB Med (Italy) plus a premium to be mutually agreed for each delivery

There is no escalation/de-escalation for density to be applied to this price."
