SALE AND PURCHASE AGREEMENT
Contract reference: Original SPA (propane, DAP Takoradi)
Contract date: 2018-03-15
Supplier: Vitol SA
Customer: Genser Energy Ghana Ltd
Currency: USD
Payment terms: pre-payment in line with the Prepayment Schedule, bi-monthly payments of USD 2,500,000 each month (Pre-Payment Clause)
Term: 1 April 2018 to 30 April 2018, extendable to 15 November 2018
Pricing:
- Propane, DAP one safe port, one safe berth Takoradi, Ghana (m/t, minimum take-or-pay quantity +/- 5% at Seller's option); price Opis non TET MB Propane plus premium of USD 54 per m/t | qty 9000
- Floating storage unit and bunkering, pro rata, invoiced and paid monthly in advance | qty 1 | 12,000.00 per day

Key terms:
Determination of Quality and Quantity: "the quantity of Product sold under this Agreement shall be the vessel discharge figures determined at the discharge terminal by an independent inspector".
Cure Clause: a party detecting an inconsistency in quality, quantity, invoices, prices or any other issue must notify the other immediately and not later than three (3) working days; the other party is to take steps to remedy within seven (7) days.
Time Bar Clause: Buyer to notify any complaint about quantity or quality no later than the 45th day after completion of discharge; no party liable for any claim or dispute where legal proceedings are not commenced within one year of the date of delivery of the Product; if not, the claim is time barred and finally extinguished.
Force Majeure Clause: excludes any delay or prevention of a party's accrued obligation to make payment.
Notice Clause: notices to Genser by email on "Contracts/Pricing/Notification" to Baafour Asiamah-Adjei, Daniella Akowuah and the generic FUEL address.
No Variation Clause: variations must be in writing and agreed by the parties.
Governing law: English law.
