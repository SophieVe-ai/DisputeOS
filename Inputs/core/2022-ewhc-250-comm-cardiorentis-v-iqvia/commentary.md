# Cardiorentis AG v IQVIA Ltd and IQVIA RDS, Inc. — [2022] EWHC 250 (Comm)
- **Court / venue:** High Court of Justice, Business and Property Courts of England and Wales, Commercial Court (QBD), Royal Courts of Justice, Strand, London WC2A 2LL (Case Nos CL-2019-000064 and CL-2018-000841, consolidated)
- **Judge:** Mr Justice Butcher
- **Hearing / hand-down:** Hearing 18 October to 2 December 2021 (various dates); further written submissions 3 and 6 December 2021; judgment 10 February 2022
- **Source:** https://caselaw.nationalarchives.gov.uk/ewhc/comm/2022/250
- **Tier:** core: a service provider's claim on a schedule of unpaid invoices (EUR 9.5m claimed) against a customer alleging defective performance; shows set-off by a damages cross-claim failing, while the creditor still loses invoices it cannot prove (milestone bonuses, over-billed pass-throughs, invoices dropped from its own schedule).

- **Creditor (Defendants / Part 20 Claimants):** IQVIA Ltd (formerly Quintiles Ltd), a contract research organisation (CRO) that ran the TRUE-AHF Phase III clinical trial, and IQVIA RDS, Inc. (North Carolina parent) [para 2].
- **Debtor (Claimant / Part 20 Defendant):** Cardiorentis AG, trial sponsor developing the drug ularitide [paras 1, 16, 20].

### Amounts
- Invoiced and unpaid (as claimed): EUR 9,501,587.25 (also stated as EUR 9,501,587.24) plus interest [paras 225, 468].
- Reductions conceded or established: EUR 181,319.10 (invoices dropped from the witness schedule) [para 469]; EUR 1,078,896 (pass-through charges within budgeted fees, conceded at trial) [para 470].
- Disputed milestone invoices: EUR 2,332,579.80 and EUR 1,399,547.88 [paras 472, 475].
- Debtor's cross-claim: damages for wasted expenditure on the trial (amount not stated in the judgment) [paras 245-246]; North Carolina statutory treble damages claim against IQVIA RDS, Inc. [para 248]; injunction for data access [para 249].
- Interest: claimed; contractual rate available under GSA cl 2 (2% above BoE one-month base rate per month on undisputed sums) [para 27]; award left for further submissions [para 482].

### Debtor's defences and complaints
1. **Set-off of damages for defective performance**: IQVIA failed to train investigators and monitor sites, so ineligible patients were enrolled and the trial (results unblinded in early 2016) was rendered unreliable and the expenditure wasted. Proceedings issued in North Carolina on 23 Mar 2018 and in England on 1 Feb 2019, after IQVIA sued on the invoices on 31 Dec 2018 [paras 222-226, 231, 244-246, 468].
2. **Put to proof generally** on the invoices claimed [para 468].
3. **Milestone not met: 90% data clean by 29 Feb 2016** (invoice THV-158125) [paras 472-474].
4. **Milestone not met: top-line result within 13 days of Database Lock** (invoice THV-159368), because the TLFs of 24 Mar 2016 were corrected on 19 Apr 2016 [paras 475-477].
5. **Over-billed pass-through costs** that fell within budgeted fees [para 470].

## Outcome
### Decision on each defence
1. **Rejected.** Cardiorentis did not show that any identifiable or significant number of protocol deviations resulted from IQVIA's breach [para 396]. The study was reliably negative because the drug did not work, not because of ineligible patients [paras 442, 457], so no wasted expenditure was shown [para 460]. On any plausible view the trial would not have been profitable anyway [para 463 onward]. The damages claim failed, leaving nothing to set off [para 483].
2. **Partly upheld.** IQVIA was held to its own witness schedule, so the four omitted invoices (EUR 181,319.10) were excluded for want of evidence [para 469]. The balance was proved absent specific challenge [para 481].
3. **Upheld.** IQVIA's evidence was "markedly less extensive and detailed" than expected. The weekly report was not directed at the milestone and its own internal emails pointed the other way, so the milestone was not proved [paras 473-474].
4. **Upheld.** On construction, the bonus required accurate figures within 13 days; any later correction meant the milestone was not met, even though the p-value change did not alter the result [paras 478-480].
5. **Upheld (conceded).** EUR 1,078,896 deducted [para 470].
- Separately, Cardiorentis's claim to an injunction for access to the eTMF data succeeded; delivery up of other study materials was conditional on payment under cl 8 [paras 465-467, 483].

### Money result
- IQVIA awarded a principal sum of EUR 4,509,244.47 on its counterclaim [paras 482-483]. This equals 9,501,587.25 - 181,319.10 - 1,078,896 - 2,332,579.80 - 1,399,547.88.
- No set-off: Cardiorentis's damages claim was dismissed [para 483].
- Interest: left for further submissions ("what if any interest should be paid") [para 482]. Costs: not stated. Form of order: not stated.

## Commentary
*(Commentary: author's analysis, not the court's.)*
- What decided the debt claim was proof, invoice by invoice. Ordinary fee and pass-through invoices were recovered without difficulty once the cross-claim failed. The creditor lost about EUR 5m of its EUR 9.5m claim through its own evidential gaps: a witness schedule that left out four invoices, a pass-through over-billing concession, and two conditional milestone invoices for which it could not prove the trigger.
- For collections: invoices that depend on a performance condition (milestones, earn-backs, bonuses) need contemporaneous evidence aimed at that condition. A generic progress report, or a client's informal "milestone met" email that is not pleaded as an agreement or estoppel, is not enough, and the creditor's internal emails can undermine it.
- DisputeOS mapping: Reconciliation should classify invoices as unconditional (fees, pass-through) or conditional (milestone). It should check the claimed schedule against the evidence schedule so that no invoices drop out, and net off any pass-through items that sit inside the fixed budget. Challenge should reject a broad defective-performance set-off unless the debtor can show causation and loss. It should uphold milestone challenges where proof of the trigger (date-bound data-clean status, accuracy within 13 days) is missing or later corrected.
- Resolution: a court-claim pack for the unconditional invoices, with contractual interest under GSA cl 2 (the clause covers "undisputed" amounts, so expect argument on disputed items), and settlement or withdrawal for milestone invoices with weak evidence. Data access may have to be given even while sums remain unpaid, because only delivery up was conditional on payment.
