Contract reference: GSA (TRUE-AHF Phase III study of ularitide)
Contract date: 2012-08-30
Supplier: IQVIA Ltd (then Quintiles Ltd)
Customer: Cardiorentis AG
Currency: EUR
Payment terms: 30 days from receipt of invoice (prepayment, advance and investigator invoices within 10 calendar days of receipt)
Pricing:

- Key Milestone 4, Statistical Analysis (Top Line Result): target 13 days after Database Lock; 3% of fees for Traditional Services available for earn back | qty 1

Clause 2 (extract): "Sponsor will pay Quintiles the fees, expenses and pass-through costs in accordance with the budget (Attachment 2) and payment schedule (Attachment 4) attached ... Quintiles reserves the right to impose, and Sponsor agrees to pay if imposed by Quintiles, interest in an amount equal to two percent (2%) above the one month base interest rate established by the Bank of England per month ... of all undisputed amounts owing hereunder, which are outstanding thirty (30) or more days from the due date of the invoice ... Clause 8: at completion, Sponsor's materials and data to be delivered to Sponsor, subject to the payment obligations in Section 2.0.

Clause 16: no liability for loss of profits or consequential loss; aggregate liability capped at fees actually received from Sponsor.
