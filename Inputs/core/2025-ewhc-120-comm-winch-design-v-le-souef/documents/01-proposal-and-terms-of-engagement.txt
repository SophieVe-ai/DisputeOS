PROPOSAL AND TERMS OF ENGAGEMENT
Contract reference: Project 1399 Somnio
Contract date: 2020-11-03
Supplier: Winch Design Ltd
Customer: Carl Le Souef
Client's Representative: Erik Bredhe
Period: October 2020 to March 2024 (approximately 42 months, subject to any extension)
Payment terms: 30 days from invoice
Scope: interior and exterior, decorative design and other associated services for the luxury areas of a turnkey motor yacht project to be constructed at the Vard shipyard, in Aalesund, Norway
Pricing:
- Stage One Part One - Interior & Exterior Preliminary Concept Design | qty 1 | £595,000.00
- Stage One Part Two - Interior & Exterior Final Concept Design | qty 1 | £265,000.00
- Stage Two - Exterior Detail Design | qty 1 | £1,650,000.00
- Stage Three and Four - Interior Detail Design and Interior Decorative Design | qty 1 | £1,495,000.00
- Stage Five - Estimated Interior Design Management | qty 1 | £704,000.00
- Stage Five - Estimated Exterior Design Management | qty 1 | £576,000.00
- Stage Six - Procurement | qty 1 | £TBC
Total Winch Charges: £5,285,000.00
Payment Schedule: first invoice £300,000.00 (Stage One Part One) one month after acceptance of the Proposal; second invoice £295,000.00 (Stage One Part One) two months after acceptance; third invoice £265,000.00 (Stage One Part Two) three months after acceptance.
Terms of Engagement (Annex I):
- Clause 1.1: "the Client" means "the person named as Client in the Proposal".
- Clause 5.1: "Winch will have no obligation to provide any of the Services until it has received payment of the first instalment of the Total Winch Charges in accordance with the Proposal".
- Clause 5.2: "Winch will issue invoices for subsequent instalments of the Total Winch Charges as such instalments fall due in accordance with the Timetable".
- Clause 5.6: "In respect of Fees, payment is due within thirty (30) days of the date of Winch's invoice". Interest on late sums at 3% per annum above Base Rate.
- Clause 11.1: either Party may terminate by written notice if the other "commits a material breach of the Contract which, if remediable, is not remedied within thirty (30) days of such Party receiving written notification of such breach, requiring its remedy".
- Clause 12.1: "If Client fails to make punctual payment to Winch of any payments referred to in the Contract and such failure is not remedied within fifteen (15) days of written notice by Winch to Client, Winch may (i) suspend the provision of the Services until such time as payment has been made and (ii) adjust the Timetable accordingly".
Signature Page: "The Client accepts the terms of the Proposal set out above and acknowledges a copy of the Terms of Engagement of Winch".
Signed: Ms Grapin for Winch Design Ltd on 2020-11-04; "Signed by Erik Bredhe on behalf of Carl Le Souef"
