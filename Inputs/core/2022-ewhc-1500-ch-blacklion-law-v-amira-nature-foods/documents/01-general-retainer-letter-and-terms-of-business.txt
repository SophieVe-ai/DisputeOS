GENERAL RETAINER LETTER
Contract reference: General retainer (with Terms of Business)
Contract date: 2016-11-02
Supplier: Blacklion Law LLP
Customer: Amira Nature Foods Ltd
Payment terms: invoices payable immediately upon receipt; interest at 1.5% per month from 30 days after the date of an unpaid invoice (Terms of Business clause 35)
Pricing:
- Minimum retainer fee, three months, subject to review | qty 3 | £25,000.00 per month
- Partner hourly rate (reduced) | qty 1 | £600.00 per hour
- Assistants and paralegals hourly rate | qty 1 | £150.00 per hour

Extracts:
"I enclose our Terms of Business that apply to all the services which we provide to you as our client."
"The Company will be invoiced for our fees monthly by reference to the time spent in the previous month. ... Our invoices are payable immediately upon receipt."
Satisfaction of Invoices: "At our discretion, we have agreed that payment of the Firm's invoices may be satisfied by way of payment in the equivalent value of treasury shares of the Company ... We understand from you that we will be able to sell the Shares freely in the open market at any time subject to regulatory nominal closed trading periods. In the event that there is any shortfall in the value of the Shares once sold to satisfy the Firm's invoices, the Company agrees to issue further Shares immediately upon notification of the same to satisfy full payment on the relevant invoice."
Terms of Business clause 1: "These terms will apply to all dealings between us unless supplemented or varied by other terms of business issued by us, or otherwise varied in writing."
Terms of Business clause 35: interest at 1.5% per month from 30 days after the date of the unpaid invoice.
Signed: Negar Yazdani (Blacklion Law LLP); countersigned by Karan Chanana for Amira Nature Foods Ltd
