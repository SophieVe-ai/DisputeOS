HOSTING AGREEMENT 2006 FIFPRO WORLD XI PLAYER AWARDS
Contract reference: not stated
Contract date: 2006-05-10
Supplier: RAM Media Limited
Customer: The Ministry of Culture of the Hellenic Republic (Secretariat General of Sport)
Other party: Parallel Media Group PLC (PMG)
Currency: EUR
Payment terms: Hosting fee of EUR 4,000,000.00 guaranteed by MoC in instalments "upon presentation of the appropriate invoices" (Cl I 3(b)). Cl I 4a): "Payment of the hosting fee ... shall be made in full in Euros free and clear of any and all set-offs, deductions, withholdings or taxes levied by the local Greek tax authorities".
Pricing:
- First instalment (30%), within 10 days of signature, upon presentation of an equal amount Bank guarantee | qty 1 | 1,200,000.00
- Second instalment (30%), Friday 30 August 2006 | qty 1 | 1,200,000.00
- Third instalment (30%), Friday 15 October 2006 | qty 1 | 1,200,000.00
- Fourth instalment (10%), within 10 days of the end of the 2006 FIFPro Awards | qty 1 | 400,000.00
Grant (Cl I 1): RAM, as exclusive licensee of the commercial rights in the 2006 FIFPro Awards, grants MoC the right to host the Awards in Athens (due to take place on 6 November 2006).
MoC obligations (Cl I 3(a), (c)): procure the use, free of charge, of a suitable venue in Athens; procure a host broadcaster in Greece to produce the Recording at the cost of MoC or the host broadcaster.
RAM obligations (Cl IV 1): "RAM (in co-operation with PMG) shall use its best endeavours to ensure that the 2006 FIFPro Awards are of the highest standard ... RAM shall use its best endeavours to secure the attendance of at least six (6) of the players who have been selected to win awards at the event."
Cancellation (Cl IV 2): RAM to insure against cancellation "due to acts, events, omissions or accidents which are beyond the reasonable control of any of the parties hereto" and, if the Awards are cancelled, repay to MoC within 30 days all instalments paid.
Termination (Cl V 2): by written notice for a material breach which cannot be remedied, or a material or repeated breach not remedied within 30 days of written notice requiring remedy.
Governing law (Cl VIII): English law; exclusive jurisdiction of the English Courts.
Signed: not stated (Mr Manley, company secretary of RAM Investment Group PLC, signed for PMG)
