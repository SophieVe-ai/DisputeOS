SUBCONTRACT FOR THE SOFT YOKE MOORING SYSTEM
Contract reference: not stated
Contract date: 2007-03-26
Supplier: Mercon Steel Structures BV
Customer: Bluewater Energy Services BV
Currency: EUR
Payment terms: not stated
Scope: fabrication of a tower based soft yoke mooring system (SYMS) for the Yuri Korchagin Field in the Caspian Sea. Part of the work is paid on a lump sum price, part is re-measured and part is reimbursable.

Within that price: provisional sum for re-measured work 5,373,670.68; provisional sum for reimbursable work 2,137,320.00.
Options: Option 5 (transport from Gorinchem to Astrakhan and Baku) exercised 2007-03-29, later formalised as VO-005 granting extensions of 51 to 122 days to Milestones B4.1, B4.2 and B4.3.
